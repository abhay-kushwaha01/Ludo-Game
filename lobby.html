Quick Guide
Getting Started
  1. Choose to create or join a room
  2. Wait for 2-4 players to join (or play vs AI)
  3. Click START GAME when ready
  4. Roll the dice and move your tokens
  5. First player to finish all tokens wins!
Game Rules
  • Dice Roll: Determines token movement
  • Rolling 6: Place new token or roll again
  • Safe Squares: Stars (★) - can't be captured
  • Capturing: Land on opponent = send them home
  • Multiple Tokens: Stack = can't be captured
